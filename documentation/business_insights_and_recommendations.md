# Example Business Insights & Recommendations

These are analytical examples from the synthetic dataset and should be validated again after the final Power BI build.

- Compare department encounter volume with waiting time to identify high-demand bottlenecks.
- Track appointment no-show and cancellation rates by department and appointment type.
- Review high-cost encounters alongside length of stay and department workload.
- Monitor new vs returning patient mix to understand demand patterns.
- Use operational priority flags to focus reporting attention on long-wait or high-cost records. This is not clinical triage.
